# Cross-task practice lessons (shared by all practice agents)

General lessons from the practicers so far (2026-10-16). Task-specific recipes live in each run's `examples/` and `NOTES.md`. **Append** a short line here when you learn something that would help on a *different* task; keep task-specific detail in your own run.

When stuck after a few similar failures, compare the same fine skill plus neighboring segments across2–3 different human demos, with dense RGB + aligned proprio; tabulate human-vs-own differences in NOTES, change a concrete difference, and retry. Keep trying fresh episodes/new instances even before Q1—no success ladder or boundary handshake.

## Grounding before moving
- **A closing push can retain a handle.** In gloves TRAIN206 door drill, a rim press looked seated but closed-finger withdrawal reopened the door. Explicit opening at bounded measured joints before withdrawal avoided that observed failure in the corrective sequence. Retention mechanism remains unproven; verify closure after release, not while the hand is pressing.
- **Transparent panels mislead depth.** Gloves TRAIN206 zero-shot finished Q1 at11158/12353 steps, but door closure dominated corrections. Glass pixels returned geometry behind the panel; repeated unreachable center targets did not seat the door. Measure opaque rim, reposition for a reachable right-side rim press toward the appliance plane, withdraw and verify flush. Only subsequent switch-green plus finish-native predicates established completion; near-closed appearance was insufficient.
- Visual proximity is not native `nextto`: public BEHAVIOR predicate uses both AABB distance and horizontal collision-mesh adjacency. In scanner TRAIN10, carried/released placement plus activation repeatedly yielded Q.5; a short RGBD-grounded lateral slide achieved Q1 in consecutive full runs a14/a15. Training-only feedback is not official scored/generalization evidence. Before repeating a push, inspect actual overhang: extra fixed draws caused a13 to fall, while measured open-jaw height correction before first closure rescued a15 without regrasp.
- **Navigation does not transfer by memorized route.** Fax TRAIN11 official fork fell before manipulation after long base goals and four timeouts; initial fwd1.2 already ended with ~36deg heading error. Before EACH base move, use latest RGB + `s.xyz` to check multiple floor patches, chair feet/divider bottoms and full chassis/arm/object swept clearance. Unknown/occluded depth is not free space. Near furniture use single-axis0.10–0.20m moves and small turns, inspect each endpoint, and stop/reassess on stalls or tilted floor/verticals rather than chaining forward/compound goals. `achieved` and planar odometry do not certify uprightness. That historical early-P6 run ignored `max_steps`/`seconds`; promoted controller26efe4a **honors explicit caps including final hold** (defaultNone). Exact collision cause remains unproven; see fax008 NOTES for public action evidence.
- Read the task's BDDL goal (installed under `BEHAVIOR-1K/bddl3/bddl/activity_definitions/<task>/problem0.bddl`), the prompt, and the coarse + fine annotations, then watch the human RGB. The goal can include items you'd otherwise skip (lunch box: the tea bottle and closing the fridge are goal predicates).
- Prefer the human demo recorded on **the same instance** as your slot (pictures: episode 6806 matched its instance much better than 6800).
- Follow the human's object order and check for occlusion (lunch box: the cookie was hidden behind the sandwich, so the sandwich goes first).

## Control
- If IK stalls or hits a joint limit, command the **human's joint configuration** at that moment with `act(q=…)` and make small Cartesian moves from there (fax placement).
- Copy human postures for approach (pictures: raise the arm *before* approaching the counter; the oblique human posture), but **adapt where our grasp differs** (gloves: rotate the held glove's knuckles forward into the drum instead of copying the human wrist).
- Don't over-constrain gripper orientation; exact surface-normal axes kept hitting wrist limits (pictures).
- A stationary close on a thin flat object failed; **sweep inward while closing** (pictures' poster grasp).
- Persistent commands: a blocked or pressing target keeps pushing. Release it (`act(left={"q": measured joints})`) before moving on.
- Chain free-space moves in one script with stop-on-not-achieved.
- Endpoint clearance is not swept-path clearance: withdraw away from the object at high Z, reorient in free space, then descend and insert (scanner: simultaneous diagonal descent/reorientation knocked it off the desk).
- **Unfold both arms before deep crouching:** an untargeted hanging arm can obstruct torso motion. Cabinet TRAIN10 hanging-right crouch toppled the held target; explicit right `preset='ready'` before crouch avoided it. Check the empty arm too—later chair-side q2lag required raising/adducting it. Effort/lag do not identify contact force.
- **Preserve entry order when simplifying a contact skill:** cabinet tip repeated only after lowering OUTSIDE the opening before lateral entry; a direct diagonal shortcut to the same achieved endpoint crossed the top and stalled. Joint-space alternate wrist branches can help held-panel reorientation, but FK reachability/approximate corner sweeps are not collision checking. Two checkpoint continuations attached successfully; no native-reset/generalization claim.

## Perception
- **Inspect cavities, not only outer faces, when a cleaning goal remains false.** Trumpet TRAIN10 looked clean externally after many strokes; a central grasp/lift revealed the final black patch inside its bell. RGBD localization plus rotating the brush face inward and moving the held object toward the reachable brush changed native Q0→Q1. Exterior-only sweeps had just displaced the object. One historical TRAIN10 practice completion, not repeatability/generalization; `trumpet-run-026/examples/inner-bell-cleaning-001.md`.
- **Transfer grasp-relative geometry, not saved poses.** Trumpet's first NOTES-only attempt on unseen TRAIN278 finished native success/Q1 at 4,040 ticks (134.667 sim s; 4708.266 wall s), with no demos/restores/intermediate outcome checks. Regrasp-specific brush calibration and presenting the held cornet within the other arm's reach supported valve/body and bell approaches. The exact success tick/decisive stroke is unknown because feedback came only at finish. One unseen practice success is not repeatability or frozen-eval qualification; `trumpet-run-026/examples/train278-first-success.md`.
- **Remeasure cuffs after every failed pinch:** contact can shift a soft-looking rigid object several centimetres. Lift above the newly observed cuff before lateral alignment, descend vertically, close at measured joints, then verify airborne retention against fixed structure. Low lateral corrections swept a glove off the counter; remeasurement/retries supported two fresh TRAIN112 Q1 runs.
- Measure geometry with `s.xyz` before any contact move: edges, surfaces, clearance, floor ahead.
- **Shiny surfaces reflect objects:** depth on reflected pixels gives the surface's height, not the object's. Confirm the object in more than one view before trusting a patch (pictures).
- Compare the object against fixed structure (shelf edge, counter) rather than its position in a moving wrist image.

## Base and safety
- **Move the workspace instead of forcing a panel target:** when a left-arm door push approaches lateral/wrist limits, withdraw clear, shift the base so the panel lies comfortably at positive BODY-left, and remeasure. Push the actual panel center, not the fixed frame above it; inspect hinge motion/flush closure after withdrawal. Gloves TRAIN112 pair succeeded with this correction, but repeated miscontact cost time; command achievement/effort alone did not establish closure.
- Keep the base clear of counters and furniture; brushing them tilts the robot (lunch box, fax). A fall or tilt is a failed rollout in the official evaluation.
- After a forceful action, a small back-off can relieve a large torso load (fax: 100 Nm → ~0).

## Practice loop
- Rank the hard sub-steps, practise the top one first, reset freely, and **record every success exactly** in `examples/` (commands, proprio, depth, image pointers).
- Repeat from fresh resets before calling something mastered.
- Watch the **time cap** (≈1.5× the human mean, in sim steps). Fax's first full success took 357 s against a ~204 s cap; the repeat made it in 201.5 s.
- Fit visible surface planes from RGBD and remeasure clearance after approach: silhouette overlap and base travel can hide unchanged object clearance when the robot tilts against an obstruction (pictures; touching link not identified).
- Asymmetric finger apertures can accompany a valid edge grasp (lunch box sandwich and cookie); verify with a small lift and retreat against fixed scene structure rather than rejecting/reclosing just to make the fingers symmetric.

- Raise a held object to placement-height carry **before** entering furniture overhangs; fax a03 removed under-desk recovery and cut201.5→158.4sim s. Still check rotational swept clearance: the high arm brushed a divider during the turn.

- Include articulated doors in base/forearm clearance, not only the cabinet: gloves' open washer door trapped the forearm during low reaches and tilted the base during lateral travel; retreat to clear it before changing approach.
- A visible FRONT-surface-to-wall gap is not free space behind an object: eight successful human posters retain ~6cm front stand-off after hanging. Compare equivalent human geometry before deciding to push closer; do not target zero gap (pictures deep study).
- Match the **held object's full pose relative to its visible target**, not only hand joints/TCP: pictures p4/p6 matched hand pose but differed4.624° in grasp transform. RGBD plane/top-edge/nail-relative compensation yielded two native-reset Q1 runs; normal-only and premature wall-parallel corrections failed. On visible seating snap, cancel preload with measured q and fully release; then withdraw/lower to verify unsupported retention. Same-instance evidence only, not generalization.

- **Clearance-first pickup transfers better than a diagonal reach:** retract open pads before lateral/base alignment, then insert straight in short inspected chunks. Pictures TRAIN280 first approach toppled the poster; this ordering achieved4/4 subsequent native-reset successes. Exact retraction/insertion distances and routes remain instance-specific.
- **Remeasure the grasp transform on every pickup:** fit the held object frame from RGBD plane + edge, and aim it relative to the visible target/wall; compensate TCP rotation pivot rather than copying hand pose. TRAIN280 required fresh calibration despite reused arm postures. Transfer the geometric relation, not image pixels or base deltas.
- **Release at the observed event, not at a fixed sequence index:** at visible seating snap, replace persistent pushing targets with measured-q/full-open immediately; omit remaining pushes. Verify unsupported retention by retreating and lowering empty hand. Effort spikes/lag are neither contact-force nor attachment proof. Pictures evidence: TRAIN2804/5 overall incl initial failure, adapted4/4; near-deterministic same-instance repeats are not zero-shot/generalization evidence.
- **Transient lift is weaker evidence than retained carry:** Brussels TRAIN10 thin container-rim pinches lifted briefly but slipped on the next lift; preserve them as failed retention, not successful grasps. Horizontal insertion after lowering outside the rim avoided a prior diagonal collision, but did not by itself guarantee adequate pad engagement. A distinct right high-shelf grasp retained through full transport; scene-specific details in `runs/brussels-run-031/examples/fridge-container-001.md`.
- **Small round objects need pad-plane height, not only top-surface height:** Brussels' vertical pinch above the sprout top closed empty; depth-derived midsection height plus a short stance correction enabled one verified lift, lateral carry and board placement. Asymmetric fingers[.05,.0006] were compatible with retention. See `runs/brussels-run-031/examples/sprout-transfer-001.md`; single success, not repeatability.
- **Keep the tool edge objective while changing blade direction to fit reach:** a held knife's edge-down pose need not preserve its original longitudinal heading. Brussels reoriented safely in free space to fingers+X/closing+Z, blade+Y/edge-down, after backward-finger alternatives had no suitable public IK candidates. First cutting contact then hit a nonrecoverable simulator error; no verified cut claim. Exact geometry and error boundary in `runs/brussels-run-031/examples/knife-cutting-001.md`.
