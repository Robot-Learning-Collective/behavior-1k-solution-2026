## General procedure for a new instance (not a fixed route)

Practice results below are adapted TRAIN evidence, not dev12–19 success predictions. In evaluation, obey its own native episode/step cap and no practice resets/feedback.

Survey RGBD: locate poster, accessible side edge, nail, wall and free-floor route. BODY x forward/y left/z up; image pixels and odometry are not world coordinates. Do not replay TRAIN10/280/292 base distances or pixels on a new instance.
2. Use learned pregrasp/sweep/carry postures as seeds, not object localization. Diagonal motion near the edge toppled the poster in280-a01. TRAIN280's12cm retraction and8+4cm insertion are instance-specific examples, not universal distances.

Before copying a sweep seed, match its poster-to-hand plane orientation as well as edge position: TRAIN292 head-on vs successful oblique approach differed≈64deg, and depth correction alone toppled the poster. 4. Aim the POSTER, not merely TCP. After prehang, expose the nail by lowering hand if needed. Re-select finite RGBD poster interior ROI, two top-edge points, visible nail and wall-only ROI. ## Flat-poster pickup branch — first-class, still experimental
If poster is flat on a counter or floor, do NOT replay upright sweep or old grasp transform. First identify full frame plane, rim thickness, supporting surface and accessible boundary using unobscured head/wrist RGBD. Raise into a reachable clearance posture before approaching. A tilted thin-edge pinch is a candidate only where both pads can straddle the rim without the wrist or lower finger intersecting the support. Otherwise create access by short inspected surface slides toward a free boundary; remeasure after every slide because the frame rotates as well as translates.
