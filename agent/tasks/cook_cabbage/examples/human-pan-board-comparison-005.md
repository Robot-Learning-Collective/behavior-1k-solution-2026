## Live adaptation hypothesis
Current board contains diced cabbage AND an intact chili half, with another chili half on adjacent counter. A pour would also move that intact half; account for it rather than assuming cabbage-only contents. The board is near counter edge (possibly useful for lower-jaw clearance), but current angle, support, thickness and particle footprint require fresh RGBD. First place pan on burner, then approach a clear board edge from outside the lip. Use near-vertical closure across board thickness with a relatively horizontal finger direction; lift via a checked torso/arm path while keeping board level, then align the actual receiving pan and pouring edge before tilt. Knife may stay in the other raised hand only if its entire swept volume clears work. None of these pan/board actions has yet been validated in this practice world.
