# EVAL PROCEDURE — installing a scanner on an UNSEEN layout

**Goal:** scanner powered on and independently supported next to laptop. Use current public RGBD and robot self-state only. This procedure supersedes historical recipes below. All historical-instance coordinates (including10/237/199), joint arrays, draw lengths and timings below are EXAMPLES ONLY, never a route or contact template for a new layout. Native predicate/Q feedback belongs to practice and is NOT an evaluation sensor. No reset, restore or private object/scorer query during evaluation.

1. **Measure before committing.** Read assigned current interface, observe, explicitly initialize both gripper intents. Locate scanner, laptop, supporting surfaces, table edges, chairs and a clear floor route together. Sample fresh BODY XYZ for scanner front/rear/side rims, top and visible low lip; laptop near/far boundaries; true tabletop height and edge bracketing the grasp lateral line. Scan rows to distinguish tabletop from bevel/fascia. Record uncertainty if underside is occluded. Recompute geometry after base motion; odometry drifts and cannot prove height/tilt. Plan one accessible pickup edge and one reachable destination with room for both jaws and withdrawal. Raise arms/torso to clearance BEFORE approaching the desk; do not drive low hands underneath it.

2. **Prepare an edge only if needed; use BASE-only draws.** If scanner already overhangs safely, skip preparation. Otherwise approach from clear space, orient the hand while withdrawn, stage above measured rear edge, and lower behind it without driving into the support. With hand fixed relative to BODY, translate the BASE toward the chosen accessible edge in a bounded displacement; do not replace this with an ARM Cartesian pull. Clear upward, inspect, remeasure and reposition rear hand between draws. Scanner and robot can move together, so nearly unchanged rear BODY pixels are NOT evidence of remaining support. Verify actual grip intent after compound moves. If a draw barely moves the scanner relative to the desk, remeasure and distinguish top-only contact from rear engagement; reposition behind the rear instead of blindly repeating. After each draw measure scanner FRONT versus true desk edge at the SAME lateral grasp line; interpolate bracketing exposed top-edge samples, not a different bevel/face. Stop drawing once the lower-jaw swept path has useful clearance while most scanner remains supported. Near the edge, choose only a small increment justified by measured geometry. Do not replay a fixed final draw: a historical extra10cm ejected the scanner; a later5–6cm choice worked in that particular geometry. Horizontal overhang alone is insufficient—check scanner low lip versus lower jaw and desktop. If uncertain, inspect rather than blindly draw farther.

3. **Grasp shallowly, with wrist transition clear.** Withdraw base/hand far enough that the entire wrist/jaw sweep clears scanner and table BEFORE opening/rotating into a roughly vertical jaw pair. Descend in free space, then approach the exposed rim separately. Ground desired TCP from actual pad endpoints/pinch geometry: lower jaw below low rim and outside desktop, upper jaw above rim. Insert only far enough to straddle; unnecessary depth can push scanner back or off the edge. If scanner visibly shifts, withdraw open and remeasure; do not close on a displaced target or automatically add another draw. If an arm goal stalls, replace its persistent target with legal measured joint q before new contact. Close at achieved pose only with visual straddling evidence. Lift to clear support, withdraw, and verify scanner follows through lift AND withdrawal. If a close-in vertical lift hits an elbow limit, inspect the achieved pose and object: a small extension while rising can clear that joint geometry if the measured path is free; alternatively reposition the base/torso safely. Do not assume failed retention or replay the same unreachable lift. Finger gap, effort, reached pose and rigid TCP alone do not prove retention.

4. **Carry from fresh geometry.** Keep scanner raised clear of desk/obstacles; choose a short clear base route with arms inside safe swept volume. Reobserve laptop and destination support after turning/translation. Include the carried scanner footprint and its offset from gripper, not just TCP. If destination is near reach/joint limits, adjust base in clear space before lowering instead of accumulating infeasible arm goals. Never retry a guard stop blindly: inspect returned safety detail and current scene, unload blocked goals and change the plan.

5. **Place with adjacency margin, then release without dragging.** Aim alongside a laptop SIDE with substantial parallel horizontal overlap and a small positive edge gap—not corner-to-corner and not merely visually near. Estimate both footprints in the current BODY frame; choose closer lateral placement with clearance, avoiding collision with laptop. Lower until scanner is visibly supported while still retained. Replace downward preload with achieved measured joint q. Allow actual physics time for settling; observe alone does not advance it, and `seconds` is an upper cap, not guaranteed dwell—check elapsed ticks. Open on support, withdraw LEVEL until BOTH jaws clear, then raise. Compare scanner/laptop/support geometry before and after opening, level withdrawal and subsequent switch clearance. Historical releases rotated/slid despite native Q1, so do not assume opening succeeded without movement. If gap widens or overlap disappears, prefer a deliberate retained re-placement over dragging an ungrasped scanner. RGB proximity is only evidence, never proof of native nextto. Keep correction budget.

6. **Switch from fresh measurements; promptly clear preload.** With object stably supported, find the small power button/indicator in RGB. Cross-localize RGBD in a second camera if visible, particularly when depth may hit a rim instead of the button. Transition wrist well above/clear of objects. Verify actual gripper command/state before contact: a stalled compound pose+close may leave the hand open; issue explicit empty-hand closure in clear space if needed. Position suitable fingertip/closed empty hand above button; approach along the visible button normal with a short bounded press, not a deep unobserved plunge. Observe red→green. A press may stop before commanded depth because of contact; promptly replace blocked target by withdrawing to clear high space. Confirm green persists AND scanner remains supported beside laptop after withdrawal. Acquisition itself can activate the button: inspect the indicator after closure/lift. If already green, do not toggle it off. Ordering may change if an accessible switch can safely be pressed earlier, but final placement stability still matters.

7. **Use budget productively; never stop merely because one tactic failed.** Official allowance6387 physics steps at30Hz =212.9 simulated seconds; human mean4258.535 steps. Keep cumulative ticks visible. Familiar237 full successes took about70–80sim seconds; this is NOT an unseen-layout time prediction or wall-time parity. Reserve meaningful time for placement/settling/press/verification; avoid repeated long infeasible targets, unnecessary base sweeps and repeated observation-only deliberation. Continue task-directed recovery while budget and safety allow; a failed grasp/slide is not a reason to give up. Stop only for explicit user stop, actual budget/terminal condition or genuine safety/authorization blocker. Do not force motion through guards or exceed budget under the slogan “never stop.” Finish with honest visual evidence, not scorer access or invented success. Practice finish feedback may evaluate this procedure, but must never be used as an eval sensor.
