If the cornet drifts or the hand/rim blocks the brush, grasp a measured clear tube/central region, verify its lift, and reposition the cornet into the brush's reachable workspace. If the long bell lies beyond the brush's reach, translate the retained cornet into that arm's workspace with the brush clear, rather than repeatedly forcing an infeasible approach. Inspect the whole tool/forearm route, not just its endpoint. Inspect and address remaining mouth-end, outer/underside loops, grasp-occluded areas and bell interior—not just already-clean-looking top faces.
